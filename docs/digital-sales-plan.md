# Digital sales direction · 30 October 2026

The homepage now sells the existing digital services directly to hotel bar decision makers. The buyer can request a QR Menu or a QR Menu + Screens project without first buying consultancy. Screen content alone remains available. The audit remains the entry into operational consulting.

## What changed and why

- Lead with a guest-facing use: discover a drink, read the menu in the right language, then order through the team.
- Show a genuine own-operation screen photo above the fold. It is evidence that Cheers uses digital content, not evidence of a particular revenue result or a consulting customer.
- Give the buyer two concrete starting points, scope examples, and a package-specific WhatsApp draft.
- Explain the next three steps, with a written scope and customer approval before publication.
- Retain the existing hotel audit and events routes. The audit guarantee now explicitly concerns estimated opportunities identified rather than revenue achieved.
- Translate the whole digital path into Portuguese, Spanish and English, including metadata, contact introduction and questions.

## Source videos

Reviewed through the original English auto-generated captions exported from their YouTube watch pages; no claim of having listened to or watched every frame.

1. [Watch This If You Have a Service Business](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqo0lVveh98&t=49s). Diagnose the actual growth constraint, understand acquisition and delivery economics, and avoid a labour-heavy service priced like a low-cost subscription. Digital work must have a bounded scope; higher-touch hotel consulting remains a separate service.
2. [Building a $1,000,000 Business for a Stranger in 69 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wr6n_zNKvMk). Choose a buyer, simplify the offer, and work through cash collected versus fulfilment cost before increasing acquisition. Applied as two clear project options and one direct enquiry route, not as copied prices or revenue predictions.
3. [If I could start over in business, I’d do this sooner](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8QfAfCbOXs). The topic is culture and performance standards, rather than digital marketing. The useful application here is a consistent delivery standard: correct menu information, agreed formats, checks and client approval. No staffing decisions are proposed by the website.

## Commercial decisions still needing operating data

Do not publish arbitrary package prices or launch a subscription based only on the videos. First cost the actual time spent on menu setup, translations, visual creation, revisions, hosting, support and updates. Quote setup separately from any defined recurring service. Establish the minimum profitable scope and contribution margin, then consider publishing fixed entry prices once those numbers are known.

For every proposal specify menu count, item count, languages, visual count, formats, screens, revisions, customer-supplied material, dates, ownership/access, hosting, maintenance, support and any third-party charges. Check the display equipment before promising compatibility. The real screen example does not imply live sports data, channels, live financial data, hardware or POS integration are included in a visual-content project.

## First measurement cycle

Use the existing website and WhatsApp route. Record each enquiry's source, requested package, qualified status, quote value, outcome, cash collected and hours required to deliver. Review lead-to-quote rate, quote-to-sale rate, contribution per project and turnaround time. Fix the largest observed constraint before adding acquisition channels.

For an installed client project, agree a comparison period and track orders of featured products, average order value and product margin using available till data. Note seasonality and changes in footfall, prices and service. Website changes alone are not evidence of incremental revenue.
