# Master Prompt: Cheers Hospitality - Premium B2B Growth Platform

**Role:** You are a world-class Web Designer, CRO Expert, and Hospitality Growth Strategist.
**Mission:** Build a high-conversion, premium B2B homepage for "Cheers Hospitality", repositioning them from a "mobile bar service" to a "Hospitality Performance Partner".

---

## 1. STRATEGIC POSITIONING & TONE
- **Positioning:** Hospitality Performance Partner. Focus on operational excellence, engineering of profit, and protection of standards for hotels, restaurants, and luxury events.
- **Tone:** Professional, authoritative, direct, and commercially sharp. No fluff. No generic SaaS verbs.
- **Narrative:** "We don't just serve drinks; we engineer high-performance bar operations and digital infrastructures that maximize profitability."

---

## 2. VISUAL IDENTITY (LIGHT LUXURY)
- **Background:** `#FCFCFA` (Warm Off-White).
- **Text:** `#111111` (Charcoal Black).
- **Accents:** `#c5a059` (Refined Gold/Bronze) used sparingly for lines, icons, and focus points.
- **Typography:**
  - **Headings:** `Playfair Display` (Serif). Use for emotional impact and authority.
  - **Body:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Sans-Serif). Use for clarity and performance data.
- **Aesthetic:** Editorial Luxury. High negative space, minimalist borders, math-perfect padding, and refined grayscale imagery with high-contrast hover states.

---

## 3. CORE ARCHITECTURE & CRO
- **Hero Section:** Clear authoritative headline + specific subheadline focused on business outcome (Profit/Standards). Primary CTA: "Início da Transformação".
- **Social Proof:** Logos of premium partners (Hotels/Groups).
- **The Three Pillars (B2B Offers):**
  1. **Event Logistics:** High-performance bar operations for elite events.
  2. **B2B Consulting:** Operational SOPs, menu engineering, and margin optimization.
  3. **Digital Solutions:** Custom hospitality tech, reservation systems, and performance dashboards.
- **Strategic CTA:** Mid-page and Footer "Diagnostics" or "Project Partnership" prompts.

---

## 4. COPYWRITING GUIDELINES
- **Headlines:** Use terms like "Engenharia", "Performance", "Rigor", "Rentabilidade".
- **Anti-Slop:** Avoid "Supercharge your business" or "Empower your team". Use "Maximize a margem operativa" or "Proteja os seus standards de serviço".

---

## 5. DESIGN SYSTEM SPECS
- **Border Radius:** `2.5rem` (40px) for cards/images.
- **Button Style:** Pills (Rounded-full) with high-contrast backgrounds.
- **Spacing:** Rhythmic spacing with generous padding between sections.
- **Shadows:** Very subtle, soft shadows (`rgba(0,0,0,0.02)`) to suggest depth without weight.
